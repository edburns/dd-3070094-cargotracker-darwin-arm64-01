The binding resolutions are:

- The application operation already performs the aggregate mutation by replacing the route specification; the facade must only delegate.
- Add `void changeDeadline(String trackingId, Date arrivalDeadline)` to `BookingServiceFacade`.
- `DefaultBookingServiceFacade` converts the identifier with `new TrackingId(trackingId)` and passes the same `Date` to `BookingService.changeDeadline(...)`.
- Do not introduce a command DTO, formatted-string deadline parameter, domain type in the JSF boundary, repository access, or duplicate mutation logic.
- The prepared JDK 17/Open Liberty package is the mandatory executable build gate; do not expand this issue into historical Arquillian-runtime modernization.

Research established that the historical baseline consistently uses `String` identifiers and `java.util.Date` at this facade boundary. Keeping conversion limited to `TrackingId` preserves that contract and avoids format parsing or domain leakage in presentation clients.
